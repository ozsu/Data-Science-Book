## Reporting an error

Corrections are welcome — typographical, mathematical, bibliographic, or factual.

- **Preferred:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ozsu/Data-Science-Book/issues/new) with the page number, what the text reads, and what it should read.
- **By email:** tamer.ozsu@uwaterloo.ca, with "DS book errata" in the subject line.

Please include the printing, which appears on the copyright page.
